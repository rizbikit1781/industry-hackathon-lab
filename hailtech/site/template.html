Hail protection for outdoor lots · Calgary

Know when hail is coming for your lot

HailTech watches the sky over every site you own. It gives you a heads-up in the morning when the day looks dangerous, then tells each site, by name, when a storm is about to reach it.

Monday 5 August 2024

How it works

Two warnings, one decision per site

6:00 AM

Morning heads-up

HailTech reads the morning weather: how much energy is in the air and how strong the winds are high up. If today looks like a hail day, your sites get a heads-up to have a crew on call and clear covered space.

10 to 45 minutes before

Storm alert

Once a storm forms, HailTech tracks the hail on weather radar every few minutes and works out which of your sites are in its path. Those sites get "Get ready", then "Act now", then "Stay inside" when the storm is close.

All evening

Stand down

A city-wide warning tells everyone to worry. HailTech tells the sites outside the storm's path that they can carry on, so crews are not pulled off work for nothing.
